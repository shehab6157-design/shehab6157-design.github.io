Flagship · public repoNetwork · identity · AI agents

APIS: Adaptive Protective Immune System

Catches attackers moving inside a network, stolen credentials and hijacked AI agents. It learns a baseline for every device and, like a honeybee colony, never acts on a single anomaly signal: a pattern has to repeat, or different kinds of evidence have to agree.

  • Network signals: NEW_PEER OFF_HOURS VOLUME_OUTLIER FANOUT_SPIKE
  • Tested first on traffic I captured with tcpdump in a 3-VM lab, then on LANL's real public dataset
  • Hardened against attacks on itself: alert flooding (it separated the real attack from 48 of 50 decoys), slow baseline poisoning, and log tampering, caught by a SHA-256 hash-chained audit trail
  • The AI-agent layer's false-alarm rate was measured on 576 real tool calls across 99 sessions; its attack scenarios are hand-built

MCP Security Proxy: behavior monitoring for AI agents

Sits between an AI agent and its tools. It logs every call to a hash-chained audit trail, learns each agent's normal pattern (tools, hours, payload size, fan-out) and flags what doesn't fit.

  • Tiered like APIS: one signal is logged as an observation; two or more correlated signals become an alert, narrated live to Telegram
  • Signals: NEW_TOOL OFF_PATTERN PAYLOAD_OUTLIER SEQUENCE_SPIKE UNKNOWN_AGENT BASELINE_DRIFT
  • Purple-team loop: a scope-gated red engine (fail-closed allowlist) runs simulated attack rounds, and every round is scored as alert, observation, suppressed or missed. A separate mode launches real MITRE Caldera operations against my lab VM

Bio-Inspired Solar Screen

An open simulation model of how screens use energy. It models ten mechanisms borrowed from nature, from moth-eye anti-glare and firefly light extraction to hummingbird torpor and a honeybee-style household colony, and stacks them device by device.

68%less energy for a phone over one office day (6,300 → 2,004 J)
5.5 → 31.3days per charge for an e-reader
  • Results are Monte Carlo simulations over assumed material ranges and a year of real sunlight data (PVGIS), not hardware measurements
  • Negative findings are published too: harvesting light on a glowing screen is a net loss, and a sky-cooling layer makes a phone hotter
