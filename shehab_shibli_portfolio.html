Graduation Project · B.Sc. JUST · 2024–2025

Built a full edge-to-cloud pipeline where a Raspberry Pi 5 continuously captures road footage, runs a custom YOLOv5 model (fine-tuned on 10,108 images of sheep, cows, dogs, horses), and upon detection: triggers an onboard audio alert, retrieves GPS from SIM7600, and publishes over MQTT/TLS to AWS IoT Core. A Lambda function then applies the Haversine formula to identify and alert all nearby vehicles within a 1 km radius — creating a collaborative road-safety mesh network. The project scored B+ on both Graduation Project phases and was selected for the National Technology Parade.
